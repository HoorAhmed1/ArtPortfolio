01

Comic book

ذباب وعودة

Dhobab wa ‘Awda — a play on ذهاب وعودة, “round trip”.

A fly wakes up on a plane and lands in a country where it understands nothing — not the language, not the signs, not the traffic. It spends the whole book trying to find the one place that would feel like home: a rubbish tip. Seven pages, silent-comedy timing, written and drawn in Egyptian colloquial Arabic.
